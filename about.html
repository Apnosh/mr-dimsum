Our Story

Authentic Flavors

Mr. Dim Sum was born from a passion for sharing authentic Cantonese flavors in a modern way. Our founders, with roots in both traditional dim sum kitchens and fast-paced restaurant operations, saw an opportunity to bring handcrafted dim sum to a new generation of diners, without compromising quality or culture.

At our core, we value authenticity, efficiency, and community. We're proud to be part of the diverse food scene at Southcenter Mall and strive to provide a welcoming, satisfying experience to everyone who walks through our doors.
